What is MCMC?

MCMC methods are a family of algorithms used to generate samples from complex probability distributions. Rather than finding a single optimum, MCMC explores the entire parameter space, producing a set of samples that collectively represent the target distribution.

Each step in an MCMC chain depends on the previous one (the “Markov” property), forming a stochastic walk through the landscape of possible parameter values.

Examples

  • Metropolis–Hastings Algorithm
  • Gibbs Sampling
  • Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC)
  • Slice Sampling
  • Simulated Annealing
